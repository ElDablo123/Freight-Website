Magaly Sanchez is an experienced litigation paralegal with a strong focus on civil litigation and debt recovery, serving clients at Freight Collection Solutions Law Group, PLLC. With over five years of hands-on experience in Texas courts and legal operations, she provides vital support to attorneys and clients navigating complex freight collection matters.

Magaly brings proven skill in drafting pleadings, coordinating service of process, and managing all stages of litigation—from case intake through post-judgment enforcement. Her background includes preparing motions, court orders, discovery documents, and judgment-related filings such as Writs of Execution and Abstracts of Judgment. She is also highly effective at client communication, court coordination, and document management.

Before joining Freight Collection Solutions, Magaly worked with several Texas law firms, where she gained extensive experience in collections, civil litigation, and case administration. She holds a Paralegal Certificate from the University of Houston–Downtown and is fluent in both English and Spanish. Known for her precision, punctuality, and workflow planning, Magaly is a trusted and valued resource for legal teams and clients alike.
