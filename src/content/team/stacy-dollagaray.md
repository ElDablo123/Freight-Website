Stacy L. Dollagaray is a seasoned paralegal with nearly a decade of experience in legal administration, specializing in family law, civil litigation, and collections. At Freight Collection Solutions Law Group, PLLC, she plays a pivotal role in supporting attorneys and clients in the transportation industry, focusing on the recovery of unpaid freight invoices and related legal matters.

Stacy's expertise encompasses drafting pleadings and motions, managing discovery processes, coordinating with court personnel, and maintaining meticulous case documentation. Her proficiency in legal research and writing, combined with her familiarity with various legal software platforms, enhances the firm's efficiency and client service.

She holds a Bachelor of Science in Legal Studies from the University of Houston–Clear Lake and a Certificate in Advanced Management from Babson College. Additionally, she has completed specialized training in Alternative Dispute Resolution, including 40 hours of mediation coursework, further broadening her capabilities in conflict resolution.

Stacy's commitment to excellence and her comprehensive understanding of legal procedures make her an invaluable asset to clients seeking effective and reliable legal support in the freight and transportation sectors.
