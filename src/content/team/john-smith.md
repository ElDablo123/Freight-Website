John Smith is the Managing Attorney at Freight Collection Solutions Law Group, PLLC. With over 15 years of experience in collections and commercial litigation, John has developed specialized expertise in transportation industry debt recovery. 

Prior to founding the firm, John served as in-house counsel for a major logistics company, giving him unique insight into the challenges faced by transportation businesses. His deep understanding of the industry allows him to develop effective strategies for his clients. 

John has successfully recovered more than $10 million for transportation industry clients throughout his career and has built a reputation for aggressive yet ethical collection practices. He is committed to providing personalized attention to each client while achieving the best possible outcomes.
